## Overview

An online store for hair and body care products, split into three apps:

- **Storefront** (React): browse products, cart, account settings, checkout
- **Backend** (Node.js + Express): authentication, product CRUD, cart and order processing
- **Admin panel** (React): inventory, orders and customer queries

Data lives in **MongoDB Atlas**, with payment gateway integration, and everything is deployed on **Render**.
